Financial RiskAug 2026

SPY One-Day Conditional VaR Forecasting

Leakage-safe rolling out-of-sample comparison of Historical Simulation, Student-t GARCH/GJR-GARCH, realized-measure quantile regression, MLP and GRU for 1% / 5% / 10% conditional VaR. Development choices are frozen before the final test; GJR-GARCH was more stable across the three tail quantiles while small neural models showed no consistent advantage.
